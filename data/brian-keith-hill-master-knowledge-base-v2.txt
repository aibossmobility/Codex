Brian’s work is grounded in four connected beliefs:

- Leadership at work and leadership at home cannot be completely separated.

5. Core Frameworks

The Repair Conversation Framework™

A structured methodology for preparing for and navigating high-stakes conversations involving conflict, emotional distance, damaged trust, accountability, reconciliation, and relational restoration.

The framework emphasizes:

- Emotional safety
- Humility
- Listening
- Responsibility
- Appropriate timing
- Consistent follow-through

The ™ symbol is a branding marker and must not be described as proof of federal or other trademark registration.

PAPA Methodology

PAPA stands for:

- Purpose
- Authority
- Presence
- Alignment

The methodology helps leaders and fathers move from control, fear, reaction, and emotional distance toward clarity, influence, relational presence, responsibility, and values-based action.

Authority-to-Influence Transition

A leadership framework that helps executives, fathers, managers, founders, and institutional leaders shift from control-based authority to trust-based influence.

Sustainable leadership is built through credibility, emotional safety, consistency, listening, accountability, and relational trust—not position or power alone.
